# Join the Gates Ridge neighborhood directory

Hi neighbor! We've set up a private recommendations directory for the neighborhood on
**Vouch** — think "Angi, but only people from Gates Ridge." It's already loaded with
local pros that your neighbors have personally vouched for: plumbers, electricians,
landscapers, sitters, and more.

---

## How to join (30 seconds)

1. **Open this link:** https://vouch.business/join/GATES
2. **Type your name** and tap **Join community**. That's it — no password, no email,
   no app to download.
3. You're in. From there you can:
   - **Search or filter** by category (🔧 Plumber, 🌳 Lawn, 🧹 Cleaning, …)
   - **Tap to call** any pro directly
   - **Add a pro** you trust (and say why with quick tags like *Fair Price*,
     *Fast Response*, *Reliable*)
   - **Vouch** for a pro already listed — one tap adds your name to theirs
   - **Ask the group** when you need someone and don't see them yet

> You stay signed in on the device you joined from. Share the link with any neighbor
> you'd like to add.

Questions? Just reply to this message.
